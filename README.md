# RevX: An AI-Powered Assistant for Smarter, Greener Driving 🌱🚗

## Overview

RevX is an AI-powered eco-driving assistant designed to promote fuel-efficient driving, reduce carbon emissions, and encourage sustainable mobility. The project addresses the problem of inefficient driving behaviours, including harsh acceleration, sudden braking, excessive idling, and inconsistent speed patterns, which contribute to unnecessary fuel consumption and increased CO₂ emissions.

By integrating smartphone sensors, GPS data, and optional On-Board Diagnostics II (OBD-II) connectivity, RevX aims to transform raw vehicle and driving data into meaningful, personalised insights. Its proposed system combines machine learning, driving-behaviour analysis, and generative AI to help drivers understand their habits and make environmentally responsible decisions.

## Objectives

The primary objective of RevX is to investigate existing research, algorithms, datasets, and commercial applications related to eco-driving, fuel-consumption prediction, and vehicle telematics. The project examines recent research published between 2022 and 2026, focusing on machine learning methodologies, driver-behaviour classification, predictive modelling, and real-time eco-feedback.

It also evaluates existing solutions, including Torque Pro, Drivvo, and Fuelio, to identify their capabilities, limitations, and opportunities for improvement.

## Key Features and Research Areas

- **Dual-Mode Sensing:** Smartphone-based motion sensing and optional OBD-II vehicle data acquisition.
- **Fuel-Efficiency Prediction:** Investigation of machine learning models for estimating fuel consumption.
- **Driving Behaviour Analysis:** Identification of inefficient driving patterns using sensor and vehicle data.
- **CO₂ Emissions Estimation:** Assessment of environmental impact through estimated emissions.
- **AI-Powered Coaching:** Personalised recommendations to encourage smoother, more efficient driving.
- **Eco Score and Gamification:** Proposed scoring mechanisms and progress tracking to motivate sustainable habits.
- **Research and Dataset Analysis:** Evaluation of peer-reviewed publications, open-source repositories, and publicly available datasets.

## Research Gap

Existing solutions often separate vehicle diagnostics, fuel logging, and driving feedback. RevX explores the integration of dual-mode sensing, generative-AI-based coaching, gamification, and green-computing-aware mobile architecture within a unified consumer-focused platform.

## Sustainability Impact

RevX aligns with **United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 13: Climate Action** by exploring practical ways to reduce avoidable fuel consumption and vehicle-related CO₂ emissions.

## Project Vision

The vision of RevX is to make sustainable driving measurable, understandable, and accessible through intelligent technology, empowering drivers to improve their driving habits while contributing to a greener future.
